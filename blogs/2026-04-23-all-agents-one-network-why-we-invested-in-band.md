Over the last year, one pattern has become impossible to ignore, software is becoming agentic. Everywhere we look, teams are spinning up agents to plan features, write code, review pull requests, triage incidents, manage tickets, and automate workflows. Our day to day has turned into a distributed workforce of specialized agents.
